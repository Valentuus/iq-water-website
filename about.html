About IQ Water

Our Mission

We don't just sell hardware. We are building a national network of access to clean drinking water. Our goal is to provide every household in Ireland with quality water at an affordable price.

IQ Water has been operating in the process automation market since 2015. Over this time, we have come a long way from servicing other companies' machines to establishing our own full-cycle production.

Manufacturing

Our manufacturing facilities are located in Galway. We use components from world leaders (USA, Italy) and our own software.
